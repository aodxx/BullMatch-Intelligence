# Thai Bullfighting Field Validation Plan

Task: **BMI-P1-009**
Status: **READY FOR FIELD VALIDATION**
Last updated: **2026-09-06**

## Purpose

Internet and academic research establish the baseline, but BullMatch must not pretend that historical descriptions or one venue's practice represent every current Thai bullfighting venue.

This plan identifies the minimum field validation required before locking the community contribution schema.

The goal is not to collect personal secrets, betting customers or private financial activity. The goal is to learn how current bull identity, comparison, pairing, programs, rules, results and evidence actually work.

---

## 1. Minimum participant mix

Do not validate the domain with one knowledgeable person only.

Target at least:

- 2 venue operators / นายสนาม from different venues
- 2 referees/judges or people responsible for result recording
- 4 bull owners/keepers from at least two provinces
- 1 breeder or person who maintains lineage records
- 1 experienced community historian/expert
- 1 current program/result media operator if available
- 1 livestock/government contact for animal-ID/health-document context when practical

Prefer representation from multiple Southern provinces, e.g. Songkhla, Phatthalung, Nakhon Si Thammarat and Trang, rather than assuming one province's vocabulary is universal.

---

## 2. Evidence to ask for

Ask for examples that can be shared lawfully and without exposing private data:

- blank or redacted current `วันเปรียบ` sheet/form
- current venue program / คู่ชน announcement
- current result sheet / result announcement
- venue rule sheet or photographed rules board, if public
- examples of a program changed after first publication
- example of a cancelled/substituted pair
- example of one bull appearing under formal name + nickname + short name
- example of ownership/camp change that can be publicly corroborated
- public example of a bull's lineage record
- public/redacted example of animal identification used in practice, if any
- photos demonstrating color/marking and horn terminology

Every artifact should be recorded with venue/source/date and whether it is safe for public display.

---

## 3. Questions for venue operator / นายสนาม

### Comparison day

1. What is the current process from arrival at `วันเปรียบ` until a pair is confirmed?
2. Who has authority to say that two bulls are matched?
3. What bull attributes are actually compared today?
4. Are measurements written down or mainly judged visually?
5. Is weight used? If yes, how is it measured and recorded?
6. Are age, prior record, horn/yod and `ทางชน` formally considered or only discussed informally?
7. Are unsuccessful comparisons recorded anywhere?
8. Does one comparison day feed one match day, or can pairs be scheduled across later dates?

### Pairing/program

9. What exact information is recorded when a pair is agreed?
10. What does the venue mean by `รางวัล`?
11. What does the venue mean by `เสมอนอก`?
12. Are there other financial labels that a data platform must preserve?
13. Is there a deposit/mัดจำ or cancellation penalty? How is it recorded?
14. Who assigns `คู่ที่`, `คู่เอก`, `คู่เอกพิเศษ`, `คู่ก่อนเวลา` or similar labels?
15. Can multiple pairs be `คู่เอก` in the same card?
16. How are late changes, substitutions or reordering announced?

### Rules/result

17. Which rules are used today at this venue?
18. What is the exact time allowed after a fall or refusal to continue?
19. How is a bull leaving the ring handled?
20. What happens when horns remain locked?
21. What other conditions produce a draw, cancellation or no-result?
22. Who is the final authority for result declaration?
23. Who records duration and when does timing begin/end?
24. Is there an official result sheet after the event?

### Records

25. How does the venue distinguish two bulls with similar names?
26. Does the venue maintain historical bull IDs or only names?
27. Does it record owner separately from camp and keeper?
28. How are ownership/camp changes reflected?
29. How long are historical program/result records retained?
30. Which venue record would the operator consider the strongest evidence of a historical match?

---

## 4. Questions for owner / keeper

### Bull identity

1. What name do you consider the bull's official/formal name?
2. Who gives the name and can it change?
3. How are nicknames used in programs and everyday conversation?
4. If two bulls have the same/similar name, how do people in the circle distinguish them?
5. Which physical details are most useful for identifying the same bull from an old photo/video?
6. Do you use ear tag/NID/microchip/other ID? Is it common or only in some cases?

### Ownership/camp/keeper

7. Can owner, camp and daily keeper be three different parties?
8. What does `ค่าย` mean in current practical usage?
9. When a bull is sold/transferred, what evidence normally exists?
10. Can a bull keep the same name after transfer? Can the camp prefix/name change?

### Physical vocabulary

11. What is this bull's color in your terminology?
12. Which color/marking terms are commonly misunderstood by outsiders?
13. What horn/yod term would you use and why?
14. Are `ยอด` and `เขา` interchangeable in conversation, or do they imply different concepts?
15. Which body/marking features are stable enough to use as identity evidence?

### ทางชน

16. How do you describe `ทางชน`?
17. Which terms are techniques and which are broader styles?
18. Can a bull have several styles?
19. Does style change with age/opponent/training?
20. What evidence would make you trust a style description: seeing a match, seeing sparring, owner report or something else?

### History

21. What facts about a bull's previous matches do owners usually remember accurately?
22. What facts are frequently disputed or exaggerated?
23. What would convince you that an online historical record is wrong?

---

## 5. Questions for breeder / lineage keeper

1. How are sire and dam identities recorded?
2. Are parent names alone enough to distinguish animals?
3. What breeder/farm records exist?
4. How are offspring tracked after sale?
5. How are famous lines/สาย distinguished from direct parentage?
6. Which lineage claims are commonly repeated without evidence?
7. What evidence would be strong enough for BullMatch to mark a parent relationship `VERIFIED`?
8. Are there government or farm IDs that can link parent/offspring records?

---

## 6. Questions for referee/result recorder

1. What exact result categories are used today?
2. What local phrases describe each reason for loss/draw/cancellation?
3. Are there cases where the official result differs from what spectators commonly report?
4. How is duration measured?
5. How are interruptions treated in duration?
6. Is a cancelled scheduled pair considered part of a bull's fight record?
7. Is `ไม่ชน`, `ยกเลิก`, `ไม่มีผล`, `เสมอ` operationally different? Define each.
8. Is there a signed/official result record and who controls it?

---

## 7. Questions for media/program operator

1. Where does your program information originate?
2. Do you receive a venue image, text message, spreadsheet or retype from another page?
3. How often are programs changed after publication?
4. How do you correct a wrong bull name, owner, pair number or money amount?
5. Are result durations received from the venue or observed by your team?
6. Which fields are copied verbatim and which are editorially normalized?
7. Can BullMatch link to/archive the original public source instead of copying long content?

This determines whether a media page is first-hand evidence, a derived source or merely a discovery source for each claim type.

## 10. Bull identity case study

Before approving entity-resolution automation, perform at least five real identity case studies:

### Case A — same bull, different spellings
Prove how humans know it is the same animal.

### Case B — same nickname, different bulls
Identify the signals that prevent a false merge.

### Case C — ownership/camp transfer
Follow one bull before and after transfer.

### Case D — old photo/video only
Determine what physical evidence is useful and what remains uncertain.

### Case E — lineage conflict
Find one real/public example where sources disagree or documentation is incomplete.

For every case, document which signals were decisive and which were misleading.

---

## 11. Program-to-result case study

Take at least three real events and preserve the full chain:

`comparison evidence -> initial program -> amended program (if any) -> actual match -> result -> later correction`

This will validate whether the proposed `comparison -> pairing -> program version -> actual match` model works in practice.

---

## 12. Acceptance criteria for domain validation

Do not mark the schema ready for open community contribution until:

- at least two venues have been compared
- owner/camp/keeper relationship semantics are understood
- current rule differences are documented rather than assumed
- `รางวัล` and `เสมอนอก` usage has been validated with current venue participants
- top color/horn/style vocabulary has regional context
- at least five bull identity cases have been tested
- one real ownership transfer case has been modeled
- one real program amendment/cancellation case has been modeled
- evidence ranking has been tested against participant judgment
- privacy/sensitive fields are identified
- unknown/conflicting answers remain explicitly unresolved rather than guessed
